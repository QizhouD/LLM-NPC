# Full action demo, played by ChatDemoDirector (F5 plays, F6 stops).
#
# Every ordinary line is typed into the chat box and sent, exactly as a player would send
# it. Lines starting with @ are stage directions and never appear on screen:
#   @camera overview | player | npc [front | side | back | free]
#   @wait <seconds>          pause
#   @hold <seconds>          pause after every finished action from here on (default 1.2)
#   @typing <chars/second>   typing speed from here on (default 16)
#   @nowait                  send the next line without waiting for its action to finish
# Lines starting with # are comments.

# Establishing shot, then the player's view of her.
@camera overview
@wait 2.5
@camera player
@wait 2

# Greeting: wave and a happy face.
@camera npc front
@wait 1.2
Hi there! Can you wave hello?

# Open question: the thinking state, with gaze aversion while the reply is on its way.
What can you do?

Can you jump for me?

# Walks to a box, picks it up, then sets it down again.
@camera npc side
Pick up the green box.
Drop it.

# Fetches a box and puts it on the obstacle.
Now pick up the red box.
Put it on the obstacle.

# Turns on the spot to face the player.
@camera npc front
Turn around and face me.

# Planned walk with the path overlay, interrupted mid-route, then resumed.
@camera npc back
@nowait
Come over here!
@wait 0.5
Stop!
@camera npc side
Okay, come closer.

# The same kind of request, worded differently.
Grab the blue box.
Drop it.

@camera npc front
Careful, don't break anything!

Thank you! Wave goodbye to the camera.
@camera overview
@wait 3
